# Localle Task A Submission Note

I designed Meltemi Rentals as a conversion-first, mobile-first landing page for international tourists comparing car-rental options in Kos. The journey begins with the transparent €35/day July value proposition, immediately explains why visitors should compare the total rather than only a headline rate, then reduces the three main booking concerns: insurance excess, airport pickup and a card deposit. The fleet cards let a visitor choose a category before reaching a low-friction request form; the inclusion strip, process explanation and FAQ handle remaining objections close to conversion.

I added repeated CTAs, category preselection, concise risk-reduction copy, server-side form validation, persistence, controlled success/error states, a honeypot, duplicate-request protection, restricted database access and a database-level block for past pick-up dates. The page was tested on desktop and a real mobile device with valid and invalid form states, including a successful persisted request.

**Time invested:** approximately 4.30 hours.

With more time, I would connect availability/inventory data, send staff email notifications for new requests and add an internal request-management view. 
I intentionally did not add payment, an instant booking engine, analytics or extra pages because they are outside this task's scope.
